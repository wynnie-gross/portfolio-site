About me

I'm a Geographic Information Science (GIS) professional working at the intersection of geospatial tech, science, and public policy. As a Solution Engineer at Esri, I help federal health and science agencies apply GIS in advancing their environmental monitoring, scientific research, and public health missions.

My work centers on spatial statistics, geospatial analytics, and cartography, informed by a strong foundation in imagery and remote sensing. I'm driven by the power of spatial analysis to shape smarter, more equitable public policy, especially in environmental justice and urban planning.

Whether it's modeling urban heat vulnerability with NASA Earth observations, mapping mine expansion for human rights research, or building dashboards for conservation strategy, I bring the same rigor and intention to every dataset.
